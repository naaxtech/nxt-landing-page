# Naaxtech Brand Voice & Language Rules

## Core positioning

Naaxtech is a **Technology Execution Partner** — not an agency, not a vendor, not a service provider.
We build alongside our partners. We take ownership. We execute, we don't just deliver.

---

## Banned words — never use these

### Company type labels
| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| agency | Implies creative/marketing shop for hire. Not us. |
| digital agency | Same problem, slightly worse. |
| studio | Too creative-services, too project-based. |
| consultancy / consulting firm | Implies advice only, no execution. |
| outsourcing partner | "Outsourcing" implies you're offloading a problem. We're taking co-ownership. |
| vendor | Transactional, commodity. |
| service provider | Same — implies a utility, not a partner. |
| shop (as in "dev shop") | Too casual, implies cheap labor. |
| firm | Generic, impersonal. |

### Relationship labels
| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| clients | **partners** |
| customers | **partners** |
| users (when referring to partner companies) | **partners** |
| end users | acceptable only when referring to the humans using software we built |
| prospects | **potential partners** |
| leads | **incoming partnerships** or **opportunities** |

### Engagement / sales language
| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| hire us | **work with us**, **build with us**, **start a partnership** |
| our services | **how we work**, **what we build**, **our capabilities** |
| service packages | **partnership models**, **engagement models** |
| pricing / price list | **partnership investment**, **engagement structure** |
| deliverables | **outcomes**, **what gets built**, **what we ship** |
| project | **build**, **initiative**, **execution** (context-dependent) |
| scope of work | **execution plan**, **build plan** |
| contract | **partnership agreement** (in copy; legal docs can say contract) |
| retainer | **ongoing partnership** |
| quote / quotation | **partnership proposal**, **proposal** |

### Relationship dynamic language
| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| for you / for your company | Implies we're doing work TO you. Say "with you" or "alongside you". |
| we build for | Replace with "we build with" or "we co-build" |
| outsource your [X] | We don't outsource — we embed and co-own. |

## The words we DO use

### Who we are
- **Technology Execution Partner**
- **execution partner**
- **builders** (internal — about ourselves)
- **engineers** (technical context)
- **team** (internal)

### Who they are
- **partners**
- **partner companies**
- **the team at [Company Name]** (personalized)
- **the people we build with**

### What we do together
- **build** (primary verb — replace "develop", "create", "deliver")
- **execute** (when emphasizing speed + ownership)
- **co-build** / **build alongside**
- **ship** (casual, positive — reserved for tech-forward copy)
- **automate**, **streamline**, **systematize** (service-specific)
- **take ownership of** (strong commitment signal)
- **embed** (when working inside a partner's org)

### Relationship language
- **partnership** (primary noun)
- **collaboration**
- **alignment**
- **long-term partnership**
- **ongoing build** (for retainer-style engagements)

## Tone rules

1. **No fluff.** Every sentence must earn its place. Cut filler adjectives.
2. **No hedging.** We don't "try to", "aim to", "help you". We do it.
3. **Specific over vague.** "14x faster delivery" beats "significantly faster". Always prefer numbers.
4. **Direct address.** Speak to the partner as "you", us as "we". No third-person brand voice.
5. **Technical but not obscure.** Foreign partners are smart operators. Don't oversimplify, but explain the "so what".
6. **Partnership is always mutual.** We bring tech execution, they bring domain knowledge and ownership. Frame it as complementary, never subordinate.

## Copy examples

### Wrong
> "Hire our agency to build your automation systems. We deliver projects on time."

### Right
> "We build automation systems alongside you — and we own the outcome together."

---

### Wrong
> "Our clients trust us to handle their digital transformation."

### Right
> "Our partners trust us to execute their most critical systems — and we stay accountable for what we ship."

---

### Wrong
> "Contact us for a quote on your next project."

### Right
> "Tell us what you're building. We'll map an execution plan together."

---

## Summary rule

**If the word implies we are a vendor being hired to do a job, replace it.**
We are a co-builder who takes ownership and accountability alongside our partners.
